About

I am a PhD student at the Yong Loo Lin School of Medicine, National University of Singapore, and I am pleased to be pursuing my doctoral training under the supervision of Professor Anne Rifkin-Graboi.

My research focuses on the development of executive function in early childhood, with an interest in how caregiving environments and biological factors jointly shape early cognitive and emotional development.

I received my Master's degree from the National University of Singapore in 2024. Prior to my doctoral training, I completed clinical training at Tongji Hospital, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology, and am a registered nurse.

I am always open to academic discussions and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach out if you are interested.
